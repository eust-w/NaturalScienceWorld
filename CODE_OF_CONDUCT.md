# Community guidelines

NaturalScienceWorld welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of scientific experience. Be respectful, precise, and willing to correct mistakes.

- Critique claims with reasons and sources; do not attack people.
- Ask questions in good faith and distinguish uncertainty from disagreement.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and contributors' time.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or repeatedly promote claims that ignore the project's evidence policy.
- Label speculative ideas clearly and avoid presenting another contributor's participation as scientific endorsement.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the maintainers.

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers. For sensitive incidents, request a private contact channel without posting the details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or block participants to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and context, and affected participants may request reconsideration.
