# Current Theory Baseline

This chapter starts the baseline summary of established and active theories.

| Theory | Status | Main domain | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classical mechanics | Established | macroscopic motion | fails at relativistic and quantum extremes |
| General relativity | Established | gravity and spacetime | not yet unified with quantum theory |
| Quantum mechanics | Established | microscopic states and measurement | interpretation remains debated |
| Quantum field theory | Established | particles as fields | gravity remains outside the standard framework |
| Standard Model | Established | known elementary particles and interactions | does not include dark matter or quantum gravity |
| Thermodynamics | Established | heat, work, entropy | microscopic connection requires statistical assumptions |
| Lambda-CDM cosmology | Established baseline | large-scale universe | dark matter and dark energy are not fully understood |
| Quantum gravity | Active research | Planck-scale gravity | no confirmed complete theory |
