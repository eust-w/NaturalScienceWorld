# Dimensions

Dimensions are not a single concept. The hyperbook treats them as a family of modeling
choices.

## Dimension Types

| Type | Meaning | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spatial dimension | Independent directions in physical space | Usually three at human scale |
| Time dimension | Ordering and interval structure of change | One time dimension in standard relativity |
| Spacetime dimension | Unified geometric structure | General relativity uses a four-dimensional manifold |
| Phase-space dimension | Coordinates needed to describe state | Often much larger than physical space |
| Hilbert-space dimension | Degrees of freedom in quantum states | Can be finite or infinite dimensional |
| Information dimension | Coordinates of description, entropy, or inference | Useful for models of observers and measurement |
| Compact or extra dimension | Additional physical dimensions in some theories | Active research or speculative depending on context |

## Working Principle

When a chapter says "dimension," it should specify the dimension type. A physical
dimension, a mathematical state dimension, and an information coordinate are not
interchangeable.
