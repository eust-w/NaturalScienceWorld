# Theory Map

This chapter is the top-level map for theories of the universe.

## Axes

The project currently uses five organizing axes:

| Axis | Question | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale | What size or energy range is described? | quantum, atomic, biological, planetary, cosmic |
| Dimension | What kind of dimension is involved? | spacetime, phase space, Hilbert space, information |
| Ontology | What exists in the model? | particles, fields, geometry, states, observers |
| Dynamics | What changes and by what rule? | geodesics, unitary evolution, entropy growth |
| Evidence | How is the claim constrained? | experiment, observation, derivation, simulation |

## Initial Theory Families

- Classical mechanics
- General relativity
- Quantum mechanics
- Quantum field theory
- Standard Model
- Thermodynamics and statistical mechanics
- Lambda-CDM cosmology
- Complexity and information theories
- Quantum gravity candidates
- Project-local speculative syntheses

## Demo Hooks

The theory map should eventually link each family to at least one executable artifact:

- geometric visualization;
- parameter sweep;
- symbolic derivation;
- numerical toy model;
- comparison table;
- falsification checklist.
