## Narration text

学到相对论、量子力学和宇宙学时，你有没有想把它们放到同一张地图上？

这是自然科学世界，一个早期的交互超书项目。它把阅读、可视化演示和研究资料整理连接起来。

在理论图谱里，切换视角，查看理论摘要和开放问题。已建立理论、活跃研究、工作模型与推测，使用不同的状态标签。

维度实验室可以调整参数，观察图形变化。这是帮助理解界面的玩具模型，并不是物理求解器，也不能验证新理论。

超书提供章节预览，正文与参考资料保存在仓库中。内容仍在逐步完善，也欢迎补充可靠来源。

研究库整理理论条目、证据线索和待解决的问题。项目自己的假说始终标为推测，不会被当作科学发现。

如果你喜欢科学可视化，欢迎一起校对内容、补充引用，或实现一个可复现的演示。这是阿尔法版本，本片使用真实界面截图和合成配音。

## Capture and edit notes

- Capture the corrected release candidate. Avoid old screenshots with seeded run records, misleading physics claims, or controls that do not operate.
- A screenshot montage shows interface states, not a continuous interaction recording. Do not imply a slider change was recorded or relabel arbitrary visualization metrics as measurements or scientific predictions.
- Retain status labels when cropping. For the vertical cut, capture a narrow viewport or pan across deliberate crops; do not shrink an entire desktop screen until the text is unreadable.
- Keep subtitles within safe margins and use two short lines at a time. Split the English meanings into readable cues after the narration is rendered.
- No third-party video, music, portraits, or voice imitation is required. Optional music must have documented redistribution permission.
- Verify final audio duration, visual legibility, subtitle timing, and all scientific qualifiers before upload. A rendered file is not proof of platform publication.
